Chemical & Color Benchmark

Semi-permanent dye, roughly 30 minutes of chair time. Effectively doubles a standard ticket.

Lightening service. Needs a licensed colorist and a patch-test workflow.

Paramedical Benchmark

Hair Unit / Cranial Prosthesis
Highest-ticket service in the research. Solves a real problem and creates a recurring maintenance client.
$275–$800ref.
Steam Facial / Charcoal Mask
Low-cost, high-margin add-on that pairs naturally with a shave.
$15–$40ref.

Natural Hair Benchmark

Loc Detox & Full Retwist
Deep-clean plus retwist. Labor-heavy, but the research shows strong demand and premium pricing.
$40–$150+ref.

Convenience Benchmark

After-Hours Appointment
Premium on slots outside posted blocks. Monetizes the evening gap rather than extending everyone's day.
+60–65%ref.
House Call
Off-site visit. Highest per-hour yield in the research, but it pulls a barber off the floor.
~$200ref.
Worth knowing: chemical services and any paramedical work carry licensing, insurance, and patch-test requirements that vary by state. Missouri cosmetology rules should be checked before any of this is advertised. This section is a starting point for that conversation, not a green light.
