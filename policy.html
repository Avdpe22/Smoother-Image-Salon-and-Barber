The Standard

How we keep the chair yours

Short version: tell us if plans change, and we'll hold up our end too.

01

Tell us when plans change

Four hours is enough to hand your slot to someone on the waitlist. Less notice is still better than none.

02

Ten minutes of grace

Running late is normal. Past ten minutes we may have to move you, so the guest behind you isn't pushed back.

03

No fees, either way

Miss one and nothing gets charged. Book again whenever. We'd just rather know, so the chair doesn't sit empty.

Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows — booked guests are always seen first.

Ready to get in the chair?

Booking online holds your slot and sends a reminder the day before.
