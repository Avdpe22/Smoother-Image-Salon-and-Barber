The Standard

How we keep the chair yours

Every appointment is one barber's full attention for that block of time. A few ground rules keep it that way for the next guest too.

01

4-Hour Cancellation Notice

Plans change. Give us at least 4 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule, and we'll open the slot up for someone else on the book.

02

10-Minute Grace Period

Traffic happens. You've got a 10-minute window from your start time before your slot may need to go to the next guest waiting.

03

No-Show Fee

Missed appointments without notice are charged 100% of the reserved service. It's the only way to keep chairs open for the guests who show up.

Why it's set up this way

One chair, one client, on time

Smoother Image runs on two service blocks a day with a hard break between them — not because the room needs the rest, but because you do. No double-booking, no half-cut guests parked in a waiting chair while the next appointment gets started.

That only works if both sides hold up the schedule. A short notice window keeps a missed slot from becoming dead time for your barber, and a fair grace period keeps a few minutes of traffic from turning into a rebooking hassle for you.
