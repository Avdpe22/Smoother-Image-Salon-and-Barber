How it started

Grown one regular at a time

Smoother Image was built on the idea that a barbershop should feel less like a business you visit and more like a chair that belongs to you. Mike started the shop in Grandview holding that line, and it's the same line every barber who's joined since has been held to.

Today the shop sits at 12121 Blue Ridge Ext with five chairs covering everything from precision fades to loc and braid maintenance. The five-star reviews didn't come from advertising. They came from doing the same thing well, over and over, for the same people.
