First Visit

What to expect

Walking in for the first time, here's exactly how it goes.

Arrival

Come 5 minutes early

Gives you time to settle in and talk through what you want before the clippers come out.

Consult

We talk texture, not just style

Your barber will ask about your hair type, growth pattern, and how you maintain it day to day — waves, coils, locs, fades, all of it. Bring a photo if you've got one, but it's not required.

The Cut

Precision, not a rush job

Every cut is built around your face shape and how your hair actually grows — not a one-size template. That's the whole reason for the hard mid-day break in the schedule: so your barber's not racing the clock on you.

Finish

Line-up, product, and a mirror check

You approve it before you're out the chair. If something's off, say so — that's what the mirror's for.

After Your First Cut

Lock in your next appointment before you leave

Most guys rebook with the same barber right at the chair — same time slot every 2–3 weeks keeps your cut sharp and your barber dialed into exactly how you like it. Ask at checkout and we'll set it up on the spot.

Before You Ask

Quick questions

Do I have to book with the same barber every time?

No — but most regulars do, once they find their fit. It's the fastest way to get consistent results without re-explaining your hair every visit.

What if I don't know what style I want?

That's what the consultation is for. Come as you are — your barber will walk you through options based on your hair and face shape.

Is tipping expected?

It's appreciated but never required. If you do, 15–20% is standard in the shop.

Can I bring my kid in for a first haircut?

Yes — Jordan's chair is the one built for it. Unrushed, patient, and used to a squirmy first-timer.
